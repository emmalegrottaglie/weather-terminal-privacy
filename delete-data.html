Delete your Weather Terminal data

Weather Terminal, Android app by Emma Legrottaglie · Updated 23 October 2026

Weather Terminal has no accounts and the developer runs no server, so everything the app knows about you is stored on your own phone. You can delete all of it yourself, straight away, without contacting anyone.

Delete everything from inside the app

  1. Open Weather Terminal.
  2. On the Now screen, tap the gear icon at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Data section at the bottom.
  4. Tap DELETE ALL DATA.
  5. Tap ■ DELETE to confirm.

The data is erased immediately. The app restarts as if newly installed and shows ALL DATA DELETED. The labels appear in the language the app is set to; the button is the last item in Settings.

Other ways

  • Uninstall the app. Android removes all of its data with it.
  • Clear its storage: Android Settings › Apps › Weather Terminal › Storage › Clear storage.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept by the developer. There are no accounts, no server and no analytics, so there is no copy anywhere else to delete and no retention period. The app also opts out of Android's backup, so none of this data is copied to your Google account, and the Atlas weather map keeps no cookies or browsing data.

To show a forecast, the app sends the coordinates of the place you chose, and the text you type in place search, directly to Open-Meteo. Opening the Atlas tab also sends the place's coordinates to Windy.com. Those services handle these requests, and any logs they keep, under their own privacy policies; the developer never receives them and has no access to them. See the privacy policy for details.
