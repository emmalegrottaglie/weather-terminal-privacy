Weather Terminal Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Applies to the Weather Terminal Android app, version 1.0 and later, and to the web version at emmalegrottaglie.github.io/weather-terminal.

Weather Terminal is a weather app published by Emma Legrottaglie. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains the little information the app handles and where it goes.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for, or use, your device's location. You pick places yourself by searching for them.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or any account details.
  • It does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not build a profile of you.
  • It does not sell or share your information with anyone for advertising or marketing.

Information sent to weather services

To show a forecast the app has to ask a weather service about a place. These requests go directly from your phone (or, on the web version, your browser) to the services below over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We do not run a server of our own, and we never receive this information.

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
Open-Meteo The coordinates of the place you selected Forecasts, air quality and UV index
Open-Meteo geocoding The text you type into place search Finding places that match your search
Windy.com The coordinates of the place you selected, only when you open the Atlas tab Showing the interactive weather map

Like any web service, these providers also receive your device's IP address and basic technical information (such as the app or browser version) as part of each request. How they handle it is governed by their own privacy policies, linked above.

The coordinates sent are those of the place you chose, which need not be where you are.

Information stored on your phone

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device, so it works offline and remembers your choices:

  • Your saved places and the few places you viewed most recently.
  • Your display settings (palette, accent set, typeface, motion) and your language choice.
  • Recently downloaded forecasts, kept for a few minutes to a few hours.

This information stays on your phone. The app opts out of Android's backup, so it isn't copied to your Google account either. The weather map in the Atlas tab keeps no cookies or other data once you leave it.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has stored at any time, without asking anyone: open Settings, go to Data, tap Delete all data and confirm. Your saved places, recent searches, settings and stored forecasts are erased straight away and the app starts again as if newly installed. Because the developer keeps no copy of any of this, there is nothing else to delete. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings also removes it all. Step-by-step instructions are on the delete your data page.

The web version

The web version is the same app running in your browser. Everything above applies to it too, with these differences:

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details when your browser loads the site, and may keep them in its server logs for security and operation; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site itself sets no cookies and loads no advertising, analytics or tracking code. Its fonts and icons are served from the same site, not from a third-party font service.
  • Storage. Your saved places, recent searches, settings and recently downloaded forecasts are kept in your browser's local storage for this site, on your device only. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Deleting. Settings → Data → Delete all data erases them, the same as in the app. Clearing this site's data in your browser's settings removes them too.
  • The map. The Atlas tab shows Windy's map inside the page. When you open it, your browser loads the map from Windy directly, and Windy may store its own data in your browser under its own privacy policy, linked above. Browsers keep that data separate from this site's; you can remove it by clearing site data for windy.com.
  • Location. The web version never asks your browser for your location.

Home-screen widgets

The widgets (Android only) show the same forecast as the app for the place you have selected. To keep them current they refresh in the background, about every 30 minutes, by making the same Open-Meteo requests described above.

Children

Weather Terminal is a general-audience app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
